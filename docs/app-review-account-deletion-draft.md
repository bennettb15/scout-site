# App Review account access and deletion notes — draft

**Before submission:** Enable account deletion for all eligible users in production, then verify a fresh non-pilot invited account can request it. Create a separate invited reviewer account and provide its credentials in App Review Information. Do not use a customer account or the reviewer account for destructive deletion testing.

Scout Capture is publicly listed but access is invitation-only. An organization administrator invites a user, who sets a password through the Reports Portal invitation flow. The same email and password sign in to the iOS app. The iOS app does not offer public account creation.

To review account deletion, sign in with the provided invited account. In Scout Capture, open Settings > Account > Manage or Delete Account. This opens `https://www.scoutclear.com/account`. The account page can be reached without organization membership. Select Delete Account and confirm in the dialog. The request immediately ends access to every organization in the app and portal. Cleanup of the shared login and personal profile runs on demand and completes within 30 days; a completion email is sent. While deletion is pending, the app shows an account-deletion status and Sign Out instead of a link back to account management.

Revoking organization access alone differs from requesting account deletion: the login remains active and the app shows No Organization Access. The account page remains available in that state.

Captures, reports, and activity already shared with an active organization remain available to its authorized users. Account cleanup removes the departing account's identifiers from supported attribution and metadata; previously shared content may still contain details the user supplied. PDF reports do not print the uploader's email. In the verified retained 10.11 report, the Reports Portal displayed “Captured by System” after deletion. When an organization has no active customer users, its data is scheduled for cleanup after 90 days. A verified organization contact can request earlier removal. No customer agreement is in place, and this note does not assert a legal obligation to retain the records.

Live pilot verification: the invited +4 test user completed and uploaded a 10.11 session. After account deletion, the completion email arrived, the deleted login could not access the organization, and an authorized admin still opened the report with “Captured by System.” An earlier account without a completed session also completed deletion; it disappeared from the admin list, and another user still saw the four Test Org properties. Portal automated tests cover immediate membership revocation, Storage ownership transfer, snapshot redaction and checksums, organization retention scheduling, and cancellation when a customer returns.
